# inventories

## Why this file exists

This folder is for inventories of entities, resources, and tracked objects.

## What belongs here

- lists of servers, services, accounts, domains, or other objects;
- short cards with predictable fields;
- links to the canonical document for each object.

## What does not belong here

- narrative overviews in place of inventories;
- work tasks for changing the objects;
- miscellaneous notes with no shared tracking format.
