# RELEVANT_GROUNDWORK

## Why this file exists

An index of groundwork that is useful when working with a reference store (how to find material, how not to spawn a second copy of the canon).

## What belongs here

- links to topic indexes that overlap with the contents of `reference/` / `inventories/`;
- cross-domain overviews on organizing knowledge and SSOT.

## What does not belong here

- the reference articles themselves (those live in `reference/`);
- the full groundwork catalog with no selection.
