# RELEVANT_GROUNDWORK

## Why this file exists

An index of cross-domain groundwork that is useful when keeping infrastructure documentation (synchronization, file owners, accepting changes).

## What belongs here

- links to crosscutting overviews that relate to operations and moving configurations;
- indexes, if you keep topic indexes for network/servers in groundwork.

## What does not belong here

- addresses, ports, and secrets (those belong in `FACTS.md` and this folder's logs);
- full copies of server runbooks in groundwork links.
