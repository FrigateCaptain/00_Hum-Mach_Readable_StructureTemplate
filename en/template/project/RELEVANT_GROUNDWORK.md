# RELEVANT_GROUNDWORK

## Why this file exists

A local index: which work-products from a **groundwork**-type folder matter for this folder specifically. Discoverability without copying how-to texts.

## What belongs here

- links only to relevant groundwork files: indexes (`INDEXES/`), cross-cutting overviews (`crosscutting/`), and a `CATALOG.md` row/section when needed;
- a short note on why this folder needs the link.

## What does not belong here

- full copies of overviews;
- this folder's own domain canons (FACTS, workflows) — they already live here;
- “just in case” links with no connection to current work;
- absolute paths of a specific machine and names of other people's internal stores.
