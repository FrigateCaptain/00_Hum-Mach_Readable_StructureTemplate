# GOALS

## Why this file exists

This file records the project's target state and the criteria for its success.

## What belongs here

- the main goal;
- subgoals and expected outcomes;
- success criteria and, when needed, anti-goals.

## What does not belong here

- a detailed task sequence;
- stable facts and settings;
- stray future ideas with no link to the target state.

---

## Example (AI / workspace)

> For the project “Publishing a training course on workspace structure”

**Main goal:** get the template into active use — downloaded, applied in people's own workspaces, and shared with colleagues.

**Subgoals:**
- the repository is published; README and STRUCTURE_GUIDE have passed review.

**Success criteria:**
- course participants can fork and adapt the template on their own;
- an LLM assistant finds the needed documents without extra prompts.

**Anti-goals:** do not create a SaaS tool; do not maintain a Notion version.

## Example (another domain)

> For the project “Relocating the company office to a new business center”

**Main goal:** move a team of 80 people into the new office without lost working time and without missing client commitments.

**Subgoals:**
- the new office is ready for occupancy: workstations, meeting rooms, infrastructure, access;
- the team has moved and is working in the new office;
- the old office is closed; obligations to the landlord are fulfilled.

**Success criteria:**
- no client deadline is missed in the move window (±2 working days);
- every employee has a workstation and system access on the first working day in the new office;
- the move budget stayed within the approved band (variance < 10%);
- employee satisfaction with the new office (survey after 2 weeks) — no lower than 7/10.

**Anti-goals:** do not use the move as an occasion to review team composition; do not change furniture or equipment at the same time as the move unless necessary — those are two different projects.
