# PLAN

## Why this file exists

This file shows how the project is moving and what to do next.

## What belongs here

- stages and statuses;
- the current focus;
- the next step and the nearest checkpoints.

## What does not belong here

- long-lived facts and definitions;
- reasons the project was started, instead of `CONTEXT.md`;
- deferred ideas that are no longer part of the current plan;
- a log of what was done: work that has a performer and an artifact belongs in `WORK_LOG.md`, not here.
