# BACKLOG

## Why this file exists

Ideas and improvements to the groundwork system itself that did not make it into the current focus.

## What belongs here

- proposals for catalog automation;
- classification questions (“domain vs crosscutting”);
- deferred indexes and overviews.

## What does not belong here

- active tasks of the current pass through the catalog;
- the work products themselves (their place is the canon, `crosscutting/`, or `_inbox/`);
- duplicate rows from `CATALOG.md`.
