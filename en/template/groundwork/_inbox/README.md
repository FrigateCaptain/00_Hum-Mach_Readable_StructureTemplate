# _inbox — drafts awaiting classification

## Why this file exists

A temporary place for raw notes and candidate work products until it is clear whether they belong as domain canon, in `crosscutting/`, or should be dropped.

## What belongs here

- drafts with no final name and no owner;
- dumps and sketches to “sort later.”

## What does not belong here

- settled canons (move them to the domain or `crosscutting/` and register them in `CATALOG.md`);
- secrets and personal data;
- a long-lived “archive of everything” that is never triaged (otherwise the inbox becomes a dump).

---

## Example

The file `_inbox/2026-08-02_notes_rsync_owners.md` is a sketch of observations.
After triage: either an overview in `crosscutting/`, or a link to the domain runbook in `CATALOG.md`; delete the draft or move it to the domain archive.
