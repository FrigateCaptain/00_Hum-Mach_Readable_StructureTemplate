# INDEXES — topic indexes

## Why this file exists

Topic indexes (maps of content): lists of links to canons and cross-domain overviews by subject. They do not replace `CATALOG.md`; they help you browse by topic.

## What belongs here

- index files for stable topics (`audio_video.md`, `texts_and_files.md`, …);
- in each index — a short introduction and a table or list of links with the path to the canon.

## What does not belong here

- full how-to texts;
- drafts with no topic (those belong in `_inbox/`);
- domain canon in full (leave it with its owner; here — a link).

---
