# CATALOG

## Why this file exists

A unified catalog of work products: what exists, where the canon lives, and which topic it belongs to. It does not duplicate how-to text.

## What belongs here

- registry rows with an ID, topic, entry type, path to the canon, owner area, and review date;
- links to this folder's indexes and cross-domain overviews;
- `index` / `crosscutting` / `domain` labels (canon outside groundwork).

## What does not belong here

- the full text of overviews (that lives in the canonical file);
- outdated paths with neither a flag nor a move to an archive;
- secrets and personal absolute paths from a machine.
