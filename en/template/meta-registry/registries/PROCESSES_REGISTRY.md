# PROCESSES_REGISTRY

## Why this file exists

This file is the canonical registry of process folders.

## What belongs here

- a list of processes and their purpose;
- the path to each process folder;
- the process's main attributes: owner, frequency, or cycle type.

## What does not belong here

- project goals and a roadmap;
- instructions for executing the steps of the process;
- disconnected descriptions with no registry format.
