# RELEVANT_GROUNDWORK

## Why this file exists

An index of groundwork that is useful when maintaining the workspace overview layer and registries (how to register a groundwork store, how not to mix canon with catalog).

## What belongs here

- links to overviews of how work products are organized and to the groundwork `CATALOG.md`;
- indexes, if the meta layer points to cross-domain practices for managing structure.

## What does not belong here

- the contents of the canonical registries (those live in `registries/`);
- duplicates of `WORKSPACE_REVIEW.md`.
