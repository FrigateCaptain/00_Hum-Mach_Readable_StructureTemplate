# WORKSPACE_REVIEW

## Why this file exists

This file gives an overview of the folder system and helps you grasp the state of the workspace at a high level.

## What belongs here

- a short description of the main workspace layers;
- links to the canonical registries in `registries/`;
- overview observations that help you find your way among the sections.

## What does not belong here

- canonical entity lists in place of the registries;
- detailed plans for individual projects;
- stable facts that already belong in specialized documents.
