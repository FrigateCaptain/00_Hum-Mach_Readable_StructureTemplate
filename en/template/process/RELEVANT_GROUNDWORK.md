# RELEVANT_GROUNDWORK

## Why this file exists

A local index: which work-products from a **groundwork**-type folder matter for this process. It does not replace the process `workflows/` and does not copy canons.

## What belongs here

- links to groundwork indexes and cross-cutting overviews that this cycle actually uses;
- a note on which stage of the cycle needs the link (when that helps).

## What does not belong here

- a full dump of the groundwork catalog;
- the process's own operating instructions (those belong in `workflows/` and `FACTS.md`);
- stale links with no review.
