# PLAN

## Why this file exists

This file records process improvements, next actions, and the current work focus.

## What belongs here

- stages of introducing or improving the process;
- task statuses;
- the next step in developing the process.

## What does not belong here

- standing operating instructions;
- canonical facts and parameters;
- backlog themes with no decision on their priority;
- a log of what was done: work that adjusts the process belongs in `WORK_LOG.md`; running a cycle belongs in `CYCLE_LOG.md`, if that slot is in use.
